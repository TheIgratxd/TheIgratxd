## 💜 About me

Hi! I'm **Diego Felipe**, a Computer Engineering student at **UNAM (FES Aragón)** and a Software Development Technician.

I'm interested in the space where **software, mathematics, artificial intelligence and scientific research** meet. I enjoy understanding how things work under the hood, solving problems, and building projects that help me learn something beyond just making the code run.

- 🎓 Studying **Computer Engineering at UNAM**.
- 🧑‍💻 Background in software development, including web applications and APIs.
- 🧠 Currently exploring **Python, algorithms, numerical methods, AI and mathematics**.
- 🔬 Long-term goal: work in research and technological innovation, especially AI and scientific computing.
- 🧮 I like projects that connect theory with practical experiments and visualizations.
- 🧭 I believe progress comes from **balance, consistency and adaptation**.
- 🏕️ Scout, basketball enjoyer, curious learner and occasional overthinker.
- 🌌 Always ready for a conversation about science, space, philosophy, comics or technology.
